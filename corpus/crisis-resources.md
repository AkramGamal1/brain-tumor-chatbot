# Crisis resources

I'm sorry you are carrying this right now. What you're feeling is real, and
you deserve support from someone who can be present with you.

Please reach out to a crisis line — they are free, confidential, and
available around the clock:

- **United States:** call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). You
  don't need to be in immediate danger to call — they take any kind of
  emotional crisis.
- **Outside the United States:** please contact your **local emergency number
  or crisis line**. If you can search a phone or browser, "[your country]
  suicide crisis line" will usually find one quickly.

If you are in immediate danger, please call your local emergency number (in
the US, 911) or go to the nearest emergency department.

You don't have to know what to say. "I'm not okay" is enough to start.
