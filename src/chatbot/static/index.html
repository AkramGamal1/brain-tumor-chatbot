Educational only. Four classes: glioma, meningioma, pituitary, no tumor.

What this is: a layperson-friendly chatbot that explains brain-MRI predictions and answers questions about the patient experience around them — what tests mean, treatment categories, mental health, finding support, practical life. What it is not: a clinician. It will not diagnose anyone, recommend treatment, or predict outcomes.
